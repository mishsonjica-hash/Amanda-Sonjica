## Findings of the Hyphotheses investigated

Before I could started the analysis, I had to take into consideration that the data used in this analysis does not include data set for 2025 July - December. Therefore when doing comparisons for both years, I ensured to filter the data for the first 6 months (January - June) inorder to get accurate results of what is happening. Using the full data set for YOY comparisons would have given inaccurate results given the data for 2024 is based a full year while 2025 is based on half the year so by default 2024 would seem like the best performing year if the full data is used. Testing the above Hypotheses will help the company concentrate on high-revenue/profit product lines, optimize inventory management of stores and implement targeted, location-specific projects to increase revenue and profit.

### Hypothesis 1: Total Revenue has not only dropped for Western Cape but has dropped for all Provinces.

- When comparing the data for 2024 and 2025 (January - June) period, I was able to confirm that the revenue only dropped in Western Cape province by 5.6% and not in other provinces. The rest of the provinces have increased their revenue from previous year despite these provinces generating less revenue on average compared to Western Cape.

### Hypothesis 2: Western Cape had a drop in revenue because of drop in Transaction count. This means customers are not purchasing as much products in 2025 than in 2024.

- The drop was determined to be contributed by the drop in number of transactions being processed in stores located in Western Cape. The number of transactions in Western Cape dropped by 7.49% since 2024. The transaction count drop is linear to drop in revenue per province. This means when customers are not purchasing products, the revenue dropped and vice versa for stores outside of Western Cape. So in 2025, customers are buying less products when they visit certain stores in Western Cape compared to the previous year causing a drop in overall revenue.
